History

HTML was created in 1991 by a person named Tim Berners-Lee

Tim Berners-Lee wrote the first proposal in March 1989. The first broswer/server/HTML structure was functional by the late 1990's.

That is Tim Berners-Lee

HTML stands for HyperText Markup Language

HTML was created at Cern

It was made to structure and share documents

Cern needed a better way to structure and share their documents.
